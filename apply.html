What we look for

Temperament over technique.

Technical skills can be taught. These qualities are harder to teach, and they matter most for long-term investing.

01

Curiosity

A genuine interest in how businesses work, make money, and win against competitors.

02

Independent thinking

The willingness to form your own view, defend it with evidence, and change it when the facts change.

03

Patience

Comfort with ideas that take years, not weeks, to play out.

Do I need prior finance experience?

No. Our twelve-week curriculum is designed to take members from the basics of financial statements to a full investment pitch. We care most about curiosity, work ethic, and the patience to think long-term.

How is MCF different from other investing clubs?

Our entire process is built around a five-plus-year holding horizon. We do not trade around short-term moves; we focus on the quality of the business, the strength of its competitive position, and the price we pay.
