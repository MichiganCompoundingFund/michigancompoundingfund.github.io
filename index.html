Who we are

Owners, not renters, of businesses.

Most of the market is focused on next quarter. We are focused on the next decade. MCF members study businesses the way a long-term owner would — asking what the company will look like in five to ten years, whether its advantages are widening, and whether management allocates capital with shareholders in mind.

01

Invest

Members research, pitch, and debate long-term ideas. The strongest theses are voted into a concentrated portfolio and held as long as the thesis stays intact.

02

Learn

A structured curriculum takes new members from reading financial statements to valuing businesses, judging moats, and writing a full investment memo.

03

Grow

Mentorship from upperclassmen, alumni conversations, and recruiting prep for careers in investment management, research, banking, and beyond.

Our philosophy

Four ideas we keep coming back to.

Our philosophy draws on the long-horizon tradition of investors like Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger, and Phil Fisher: buy wonderful businesses at sensible prices, then have the patience to let them compound.

i.

Quality first

We favor businesses with durable competitive advantages, high returns on capital, and room to reinvest at those returns for years.

ii.

Price still matters

A great company can be a poor investment at the wrong price. Every pitch includes a valuation and a clear view of what is already priced in.

iii.

Time is the edge

By underwriting a five-plus-year horizon, we can look past short-term noise that forces others to sell good businesses at the wrong time.

iv.

Low turnover, high conviction

We would rather own a small number of businesses we understand deeply than many we understand superficially.
